FAQ

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. Paid plans include full commercial rights — ads, client work, anything. The free tier is personal use only.

Do all models cost the same credits?

No — each is priced at real compute cost (9–12 cr per 5s). The console shows the estimate before you commit, and failed renders refund in full.

Best setup for talking-head videos?

Use AVATAR mode: purpose-built lip-sync, your cloned face and voice, up to 60s per take — far more stable than general video models.

Can I mix in my own footage?

Yes. Generated shots, your uploads, logos and fonts all sit on one timeline — swap any shot before export.

Is there an API?

Studio plans get one REST API for every model, with auto-routing and retries built in. Standard job states, standard video URLs.
